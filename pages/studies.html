Studies

I study BEngSc Digital Arts at the University of the Witwatersrand — a degree that tends to surprise people once they understand what it actually is. It’s a joint programme between the School of Electrical and Information Engineering and the Wits School of Arts. That means the curriculum runs both tracks in parallel, engineering subjects like Electric Circuits, Signal and Systems and Software Development sit alongside Game Design and Interactive Media. The idea is to produce graduates who can operate fluently at the intersection of both: people who can build and design the product while understanding why the product exists in the first place.
I’m in my third year. By this point, I've stopped thinking of it as two separate halves. It's just how I think now.
